About me

Hi, I'm Harry.

I spent over a decade in Track & Field. From being an athlete, being an official, and full circle to be a coach.

Iggy Johnson once told us "As a courtesy to the next, please leave this place better than you found it." Now I live by that. If you want to be athletic, I know what it takes. If you want to be an athlete, I know what it takes. You will leave each session better than you started. Play the long game.

Based in Richmond-Upon-Thames, I have provisions spanning the SW of London and Surrey. We can work together in your local area, but my skills are not limited to in-person coaching!
